# Streamer and Worker Simulator

A life simulation game developed in Python.

Manage your work life, energy, finances, and streaming career while trying to grow your audience and achieve success.

## Implemented features (details)

- Work: jobs by country with XP and skill requirements; hiring, salary, and job daily/monthly handling.

- Energy / Hunger / Health: actions consume energy and increase hunger; sleeping and eating restore energy.
- Stress / Anxiety / Depression: posting and working affect mental state; items/time can alleviate symptoms.
- Time progression: hour/day/month/year, with daily and monthly checks that handle bills and posts.
- Bank: deposit, withdraw, invest, and withdraw profits; investment growth over time.
- Social networks: posting on Instagram and YouTube yields views, followers/subscribers and earnings.
- Shop and items: food, pharmacy (meds), and electronics shop with PC parts catalog.
- PC / Hardware: parts catalog, PC configuration and PC level calculation.
- Skills: skill tree with point costs and unlockable advantages.
- Bills: monthly automatic payments for utilities and rent.
- CLI interface with an interactive tutorial.
